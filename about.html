Our Team

We draw from decades of experience in flux tower deployment, operation, and data processing at the Lawrence Berkeley National Lab. Working alongside field leaders, we understand every stage of the eddy covariance measurement lifecycle.

We believe in a collaborative approach to problem solving, and we work closely with our clients to ensure that their towers deliver measurements they can trust and act on.
