Our Services

We help you get the most out of eddy covariance flux tower measurements with tailored solutions for your site.

Capabilities

Whether you're looking to deploy a new tower, build out your data pipeline, or make sense of your measurements, we provide tailored solutions that meet your goals. Our comprehensive approach ensures clean, scientifically rigorous data products ready for publication, regulatory compliance, or business intelligence.

Project Scoping & Tower Siting

Getting the measurement design right before equipment ever ships.

  • Project scoping — defining regions of interest, uncertainty thresholds, and deployment time spans to ensure the highest-value measurements for a cost-effective project.
  • Tower siting — identifying optimal location, height, and number of towers for your specific sites and project goals.
  • Footprint modeling & flux spatialization — quantifying what your towers measure and how data scale across your site.

Equipment & Tower Maintenance

The right hardware, sized correctly, kept running in the field.

  • Equipment procurement — guidance on and procurement of the most cost-effective equipment for your data needs, including IR gas analyzers, 3D sonic anemometers, and ancillary sensors.
  • Power & connectivity — solar/battery sizing and cellular/satellite data links for off-grid sites.
  • Tower maintenance — organizing routine field checks to replace and restore tower parts.

Data Processing

Raw signals turned into data you can trust and defend.

  • Gapfilling & uncertainty quantification — ensuring your data is clean and working for you.
  • QA/QC and calibration protocols — correcting sensor drift and ensuring data integrity.
  • Flux partitioning — separating carbon flux into uptake and release, and water flux into evaporation and transpiration.
  • Automated pipelines — for continuous, hands-off processing from your site as new data arrive.

Science-Grade Insights & Tailored Tools

Context and visualization that turn flux data into decisions.

  • Integrated measurements — combining satellite data, soil chambers, moisture profiles, and meteorological variables for a holistic view of your site across seasons and years.
  • Upscaling & model integration — spatializing tower measurements across landscapes using weighted footprint modeling.
  • Tailored dashboards — workflows for real-time monitoring, alerts, and multi-year flux accounting.
  • Training & capacity building — for client staff and operators to interpret data and perform routine maintenance.

Reporting & MRV

Data written and visualized for the audiences that need to trust it.

  • Carbon market MRV — reporting aligned to Verra, Isometric, and other carbon credit market vendors.
  • Regulatory reporting — for fugitive emissions, greenhouse gas inventories, leak detection, and compliance submissions.
  • Standards alignment — with FLUXNET/ICOS/AmeriFlux for publishable, interoperable data.

Project Termination

A clean end to a project, not an afterthought.

  • Decommissioning — safe, efficient teardown of tower infrastructure.
  • Site restoration — returning sites to their required condition.
  • Equipment recycling — responsible disposition of hardware at end of life.
